# Rahul (oldmanrahul.com)

Specimen: *Hush* (~2,300 chars body, placed on HN). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Product announced in two sentences: "It's called Hush. A mac app that
  makes it easier to focus." Then the personal history that motivated it
  (classical → podcasts → generated noise), including the self-own: "I
  still don't know how I got anything done with two guys talking in my ear
  for three hours."
- Concrete sensory detail where it matters: brown noise "kinda sounds like
  you're sitting next to a giant waterfall (as a meditating monk would)."
- Interaction described as behavior, not features: "Right-click the icon
  and the noise starts. Right-click again and it stops."
- Commerce without shame: "Pay what you want, and $0 is fine." Beta
  testers credited with what they changed ("I eliminated white and pink
  noise... white noise is particularly ear piercing").
- Closes with an invitation, not a conclusion: "run Hush for a week and
  tell me whether it helped you focus."

## Structure

Name it → why it exists (personal) → how it behaves → how it's built
(offline, real-time, notarized) → price → credits → invitation. No arc;
the artifact is the arc.

## Not in the house blend

Short, but a product announcement with thin technical content — weak
specimen for a technical-witty reference. Kept as proof that brevity is
not punished when the artifact is real.
