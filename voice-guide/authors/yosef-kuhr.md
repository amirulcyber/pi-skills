# Yosef Kuhr (yosefk.com)

Specimen: *Doing Everyone Else's Job* (~6,800 chars body). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Thesis as sentence one, no setup: "I find it very useful to be able to
  do everyone else's job."
- Historical anecdotes with named humans and claimed proximity: "it worked
  for Intel... I've met someone from that Intel team."
- Dry parentheticals that carry the actual joke: "(of course the plan is
  already finalized for this quarter as well as the next — it's our
  well-run planning process to which we owe our velocity — but in a few
  weeks, we can discuss the priority...)"
- Long paragraphs allowed because every sentence earns its place; density
  over brevity.
- Regret stated flatly: "(If anything, it's refusing to get into various
  areas over the years that I personally regret... in hindsight, how very
  stupid.)"
- Ends by quoting the draft reviewer's objection at length, then answering
  it. Thanks Dan Luu by name. Disagreement is shown, not summarized.

## Structure

Thesis → historical proof → workplace mechanics (the long middle) →
variants (buy-vs-build, centralize-vs-local) → reviewer's objection,
quoted and answered. An essay that expects to be disagreed with and
prepares the ground for it.

## Not in the house blend

The wittiest of the set, but dense and long — the opposite of short.
Kept as the reference for thesis-first essays, parenthetical wit, and
ending with a quoted disagreement.
