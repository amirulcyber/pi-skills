Specimen: *Reversing Factorio's RNG* (~15,000 chars body). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Honesty caveat before anything else: "Broken in Factorio 2.1 – Version
  2.0 only!" The limitation is the first thing on the page.
- States the single prerequisite and nothing more: "understandable if you
  have a rudimentary understanding of linear algebra. That should be the
  only prerequisite."
- Names the trick like a chapter: "The Funny: What happens if we know the
  exact algorithm and its internal state?" Playfulness without exclamation.
- Narrates process, including the unglamorous parts: "my first step was a
  rudimentary internet research... After digging around a bit I found a
  post... 11 years [old]."
- Quotes a named human precisely (dev quote with date, forum, topic
  number). Evidence has provenance.
- "Thats pretty neat" — typo included, charm intact. (Do not imitate the
  typo. Do imitate the register.)

## Structure

Caveat → game mechanic → the theoretical question → the trick, named →
detective process (forum → binary → decompiler) → math → implementation.
A whodunit where the butler is a Tausworthe generator.

## Not in the house blend

Dry and technical, but a 15,000-character deep dive — the opposite of
short. Kept as the reference for caveats-first, prerequisites-stated,
and evidence-with-provenance.
