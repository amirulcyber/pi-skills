# Eli Bendersky (eli.thegreenplace.net)

Specimen: *How big are factorials?* (~2,600 chars body). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Curiosity-first openings, dated like diary entries: "The other day, I found
  myself wondering how big 52! (52 factorial) is, and that led me to ponder
  how these could be estimated without a calculator or a computer."
- Answer first, background after — stated as an explicit contract:
  "This post will start by stating how to do the estimate, and if you're
  curious you can read on for the math background."
- Checks the answer against reality, out loud: "The real answer is 68, so
  this is very close!"
- Casual precision: "52 divided by e is... 20-ish?", "being off by a couple
  of digits usually isn't a big deal." Numbers stay rough until the moment
  they need to be exact.
- Dry asides in brackets and dashes, never spotlighted.

## Structure

Question → estimate up front → reality check → derivations for whoever
stays. The payoff lands in paragraph two; everything after is optional
depth. Readers who bounce after 30 seconds still leave with the answer.

## For the house blend

- The two-sentence payoff early.
- Rough numbers spoken plainly ("20-ish"), exact numbers where scored.
- No throat-clearing; the wondering *is* the opening.
