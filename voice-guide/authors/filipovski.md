# Filipovski (filipovski.net)

Specimen: *Backups Aren't Simple* (~9,900 chars body). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Opens with someone else's quote, chased to its source: "I read a comment
  somewhere that stuck with me... Trying to find the source for it for this
  blog, it turned out that every other sysadmin has his rehashed version."
- Childhood disaster as founding anecdote: the dad, the set-top box, the
  formatted family-photo drive. Refuses the cheap blame ("It would be easy
  to blame him... but it takes beginning a career in tech to realise that
  there is a series of errors").
- Lets complexity accrete one adjective at a time until the punchline
  names itself: "an incremental, deduplicated, GFS-rotated, snapshot-based
  backup."
- Tangents admitted and cut on the page: "I won't get into it for the sake
  of brevity." The reader sees the discipline, not just the result.
- "So..." as a paragraph opener, repeatedly. Colloquial scaffolding that
  keeps a long piece moving.

## Structure

Anecdote → first principle ("a backup, i.e. a copy someplace else. So far
so good.") → naive solution → complication × N → punchline → new domain
(homelab, databases, offsite) replays the same accretion. Story first,
abstraction only when the story demands it.

## Not in the house blend

Witty and concrete, but essayistic and accretive by design — windy where
the blend wants short. Kept as the reference for anecdote-first openings
and earned punchlines.
