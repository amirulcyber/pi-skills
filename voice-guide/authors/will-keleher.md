# Will Keleher (will-keleher.com)

Specimen: *Small programming tricks* (~4,900 chars body). Read in full 2026-09-17.

## Voice markers

- Modest frame, hedged with "I think": "Day to day, I think a surprising
  amount of engineering productivity comes from small nuggets of knowledge."
- Every item is concrete and immediately usable: `python3 -m http.server`,
  `git log -S pattern`, `shopt -s globstar`. No item needs another item.
- Self-deprecation as inclusivity: "You might have already known all of
  these things! Or you might work in a domain that makes all of these
  little tricks totally useless."
- Closes with attribution, not summary: credits Julia Evans ("small bits of
  knowledge are powerful! and fun! and approachable!!") in a footnote.
- Exclamation marks used sparingly and only for genuine enthusiasm
  ("amazingly useful especially with older codebases!").

## Structure

Frame (3 sentences) → catalog of self-contained items → company-scale
extension of the same idea ($PERSON knows $AREA) → attribution. No arc;
each section survives alone because most readers only take one item.

## For the house blend

- The modest opener that lowers the stakes for reading.
- Items that survive alone; no cross-dependencies.
- End with credit, never with a sermon.
